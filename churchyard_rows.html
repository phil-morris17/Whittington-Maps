Areas

Row order and the plot-1 end of each row come from the parish's area plans (Areas 1–7 on stbartholomew.co.uk). Rows are fitted inside the surveyed area outlines and plots are spaced evenly along them, so a plot's position is an estimate to within a few metres. Use the stones around it to find the exact grave.
